# G1 — Signed photonic reduction prior-art reconnaissance

**Version/date:** 1.0 · 2026-11-03  
**Lenses:** A1 architecture, A2 mathematics, A6 interconnect, A7 device physics, A11 research; not an external specialist review.  
**Scope:** Explicitly a narrow literature reconnaissance, **not** an exhaustive patent or freedom-to-operate analysis.

| Work | Source | Scope already demonstrated/discussed | Consequence for novelty |
|---|---|---|---|
| *In-Network Analog ALLREDUCE for ML with Programmable Integrated Photonics* (OFC 2026) | https://opg.optica.org/abstract.cfm?uri=OFC-2026-Th3H.3 | Programmable integrated-photonic analog AllReduce, hardware and ML demonstration | Optical in-network reduction is **not new** |
| *Breaking the bottleneck in AI clusters with parallel photonic integration* (Nature Communications, 2026) | https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76764-1 | Parallel photonic reduction and a component-level collective prototype | Optical simulcast/in-network arithmetic is established prior art |
| *Practical limits on integrated squeezers* (npj Nanophotonics, 2026) | https://www.nature.com/articles/s44310-026-00125-5 | Optical detection shot noise, other noise sources, detector power limits | A Poisson-only simulator excludes important hardware noise and nonclassical-light techniques |
| *A Scoping Review on Intelligent Computing upon Silicon Photonics and Optical Network on Chips* (2026) | https://www.mdpi.com/2304-6732/13/11/1020 | Optical conversion, analog memory and ADC/DAC overhead | Conversion and memory costs cannot be ignored |
| *Ultra-sensitive focused laser differential interferometer using balanced detection* (Optics Letters 2025) | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40815732/ | Experimental balanced photodetection with shot-noise-limited operation | Balanced positive/negative sensing is not a new physical principle |

**Research conclusion:** A generic signed, balanced photonic reduce and conversion/fallback pathway has insufficient novelty evidence. The current work is a **physics feasibility screen** for a potential communication architecture, not an invention. Any later claim needs a specific distinct device/protocol feature, patent search, comparison to these implementations, and independently replicable advantage.
